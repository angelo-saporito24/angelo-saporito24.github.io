INameless Languages

1

In my gathering

In my gatheringI gather youfrom the late-summer grass:three white stonesdaywarmed,a distant nearnessin the hand.And I cast you,die stones,into the dwindlinglight betweenthe possibleand the im-possible.

2

Empty silence, sealed

Empty silence, sealedin vessels:bottomless dwelling.Un-sealing the infiniteinto the recurring,revealing the seeninto the unheard.Resounding,the un-uttered.Threshold:the pastbetweenthe coming.Tomorrow swellsin the heart,lingering

3

Around what you surround

Aroundwhat you surround,a stretching-toward.Birdflightin the mirroredge:namelesslanguages, un-uttered.Poems I recite,unread;silent sayingsto strangers,face-cloaked.I slip beneath,beneath the unfound.

4

In writings unfamiliar

In writings unfamiliar to the yearning hand which wrote them, I find you. Yet: who are you? Was it you who wrote them, and I only the hand? Or was it I, and you are what I became — a stranger, returning again to the estranged? Pages of windswept hours, tiny memories, duskpoems written in tomorrow’s dawn: I scour through them as one reads another’s letters, written in a foreign pen. Still they are addressed to me. Visit me, then, within their haunting brilliance. Nameless, therefore, I will not ask who named you.

5

French flutters

French flutters, rosy-lipped —between a laugh,a concealed smile.And a white scarf scattersthe wordsin a languageneverspoken,newlywedto soft whitepages.

6

Rootless homepain

Rootless homepain:sightless seekingin faraway places,a longing, listeningto longer, far-longerpagesdivorcedfrom one another.

Homelost farsickness:a re-turningto a place never been,a climbinto the presenceof presence gone,absent stairsof forgotten memories.

Hands outstretched,reaching in-towardsthe sky’sgrey-whiteness:a blank page,never oncewritten upon.

Numberless leavesin winter winds,an outflowingfrom an un-mastered hand.The stormstirs the Chaosthat brings onBeing:momentary moments.I drink ofthe outflowings.

IIWalking-Along-Beside

1

In your eye

In your eye,threadsmall,the one who isme.Separationwithin the grazingtouch betweenhalf-longing gazes.Within inaction,distance,growingas you come closer.Heartswell:wiltturn roses.

2

Windswept fingertips

Windswept fingertips, a gentle play in earthenhair.Beneath the foliage of years, I find you.A thousand hours of silence in sacred groves.Whisperings into a world-window. You were still.Riddles written in white sands.Wanewaxing tide: no one reads them now.Fragment-scattered pages, lulled leaves.Unto you I spoke of long-forgotten things.Two strings, drawn, become a single sound.You repeat, repetitions,singlenotes,unison.

3

What is beneath you

What is beneath you,beneath the dots,the twisting threads?A name,rising from the skin?

Dreamings wander about me.A daimoncloses them in.

I paint your breastswith colorclouds,fault-lines,rootsconnecting usto the skies.

Sand-stories written,circlingfan-flowers.The circle repeats:white of the child,yellow of youth,purple of age,lost,hiddenfrom seeking eyes.

4

In loneliness

In loneliness:a dwelling-together,a distancein our nearness.Half-emptiedby the other’s presence,filledin an absence.Nighthours.Two glass statuesposturing,un-shattered.

5

Apples of my departure

Apples of my de-parture:windfalls in the grassfermented sweet.Beyond orchard wallsthe fields,dreamt,wandered onlyin the mind.The words never spokencome loose,first leaves.I catch at thembetween childlike palms,grasping.Hands clasped,steps small,you pass,my shadow,walking-along-besidemy wanderlost gait.

6

Pathwandered footsteps

Pathwandered footsteps,never-roamed places.Only by treesorientedin eternal dis-orientation.You followmy footpaths,trodden between vinesof wintered grapes:a pathof hourleaves,uncounted.Astray-led,homelost,into an imageunknown,no longer foundby seeking eyes.The greatest weight:the weary wheelof years.

IIIStones Hallowed

1

Tear-shattered shards

Tear-shattered shards:a well in the darknessof a before-leaving,a possible never-return.Light, streamingthrough an inverted windowthat once was,that once containedmy world.

Mirrordust:eschaton,genesis.

And yet I see you,an absence in whichI still feela withdrawn presence:shadows of a memorybetween the leafvineof once-rose apples,wiltedin Time’s embrace.

2

Still days

Still days,rambling mind,silent chasingsafter lost time.Memorylorn night,infinite, surceasing.You imitate the stars?mirrorwater,reflections,as deepas they are high.As far from youas you arefrom your image:re-cessations,a coldness from the sky.

3

You see me as you are

You see me as you are:bare, heartstill.Through the silencebetweenI am of petals,decayed, dreamlike,leaf-feathered on forgotten winds.Lap-lain head,tempest-twirled,cold near bright waters,startled by upwardfalling things:I recede.I leave you the burning memories,bent into rings,buried deepwhere I cannot follow,and hear the soil re-sound.I smile, I dream of silence.Beyond myselfI have lost myself,past the hinterlandsof a once-fracturedimage.

4

A longing

A longingto be as you are,innocent,leaves brushing your hairto your shoulders.

Unearthed image,submerged for yearsin silent waiting,since I dug my first pitsin early familiarities,swelling to the surface.Half-whispering,uncovered soil,and you arise,a tree,announcingall I have left buried.

Short-lived wind-flower,fading as you reachyour zenith-bloom,buried beneaththe moss-covered hours.In your veins,so faint,you carry my songsas your echoing blood.Look,how a treewe have become.

5

A striking all at once

A striking all at once,a loud rapid whisper,a light within the eyebeyond the momentarinessof a word’s spontaneity.

And I hearresonant echoesof recurringdreams:memorythreads —lingering scars —a mind fragmented.

So I weptto wearied stonesleft widowedby the ragingof hours.

6

Windcarved echo

Windcarved echoout of stones hallowed:on the heath above the fjorda runestone leans in the heather,rain standing in its grooves,lichen in its letters,and a name,ephemeral but recurrent,worn almost to stone:raised this stone in memory of —Foreverlost voice,now you seek expression —How shall I erect youout of yourdestruction?

IVA Shard-Gatherer

I

Dusk-scattereddandelion dust:snow in landsof latefields.There, it settles;I wrest the shardsfrom new soils;in clasped hands,I hold them,as red-nosed children dothe fallen snow.I gaze; they surrender their secrets:in their prisms, always-changing,the glimmering hoursof a thousand lifetimes,childlike memories, flashing.Yet one,loudest-shining.Unto it I speak, half-silent,yielding,as if it had received my wordsonce, or many times, before:a returnof what once was spokenin longing.

Between heart-threadsof a boychild’s palmsit melts, shakes off its appearance,fills the fissures in the faceof Time,as wizened moss from a gentler hourgrows in the hollows of aged stones.Waterfull hands,thousand-houred,from one shard of World-Memory:slipping dropsthrough enclosed fingers,each an oceanof infini-tesimal fragments:Things that were,Things that will be,undistorted by what-could-have-been.Silvery drops,on earthen ground,a bowl of liquid-glass.With outstretched hands,I receive it,and drink of its emptiness,its vastness.Mouth, filledwith summersongsand the silence of a wintry death.

My eyes turn to springs,mountain streamsemptying into the basinwhere I mix my tears.What is this?As if it knew mefrom my first breath,my first whisper,my first tear.It traces me in its waters,complete:a face in its looking-backfrom beyond the surface.“In all ThingsI long to see me.”Hearing the wordsit has heard before,reflection-shatter:glasstears,three.

II

One I bury deep,deep in the dark soil of my ear:Things buried and unconscious,by hidden mouths spoken,by concealed ears heard.Sprouting,roots in the darkness,leaves in the clouds,a tree from the head.Its flowers: Time.Its almonds: Re-memberance, Forgetfulness,falling shards.In my hearing it raisesglass spires, golden steeples,and I fall inward,listening.

III

One on my tongue:the tongue, a pen.I dip it in the bowl of liquid-glassand write with wateron water:letters to late morning,half-whispers to early evening,veilwordsto conceal you,to trace you and your shadow.The surface closes.Inexpressible, expressed;nameless, many-named;concealed,everywhere unveiled.

IV

The lastinto the wet of my eye.Plunging, beyond the surface,past the face looking back,to the lakebed of sight:cyclopean.I wade after it,looking away,thinking outbeyond,into.Then I close the stone lidof a weary eye.
